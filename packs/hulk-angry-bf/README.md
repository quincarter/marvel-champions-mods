# Don't Make Me Angry! (`hulk-angry-bf`)

An optional house rule for the official Hulk (10001a), She-Hulk (01019a) and Hulkling (56029a) by Abis Mal and
Bluefire: "Don't Make Me Angry!" (Permanent. Setup.), one copy per hero, since an identity-specific card names one
hero. A deck uses the rule by listing its hero's copy (`optionalForIdentity`): it starts in play, and the official
precons and every other deck stay legal without it. Starter deck: Hulk's Lab Partners - Protection (40 cards plus the
house-rule card). See `CREDITS.md` and `PERMISSION.md`.

Needs the game at 0.52.0 or later (`requires.app`): the after-hand-discard event (game issue #922).

The Response ("After an identity-specific card is discarded by an ability, tuck that card under here from your
discard pile. (Limit twice per phase.)") is one Response with one limit, for a card discarded by a card's ability from
your hand (Enraged, Experimental Research), your deck (Abomination, Electro's "discard until") or play: the discard
happens and the discarding ability sees it, then the card is tucked from the discard pile.
